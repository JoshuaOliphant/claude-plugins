The cause lives in `ledgerflow/feeds/client.py`, `FeedClient.open_cursor`: to respect a (plausible) gateway cap on concurrent cursors it caches `PageCursor` objects by `(feed, since)` and hands back the cached cursor whenever it is not `closed`. A cursor only becomes `closed` after it has been driven past its last page, so a cursor that was abandoned part-way — by the validator's `islice(source.records(), sample_size)` preflight, or by a transient `TransientFeedError` mid-stream that makes `retry_call` re-invoke `ImportPipeline._stage` — is reused, and the "fresh" `FeedSource.records()` generator silently resumes at the page after the last one fetched, dropping the earlier pages (34 records with validation, one or more pages after a retry). A reader following the symptom's path (`cli.main` -> `app.run_import` -> `ImportPipeline.import_source` -> `retry_call`/`Validator.preflight` -> `FeedSource.records`) sees a retry helper that correctly calls a zero-argument factory per attempt, a preflight that calls `source.records()` again, and a `records()` that "opens" a cursor on every call; nothing local is wrong, and the reuse is two hops further down in what reads like connection pooling. Running a repro and counting pages fetched (or comparing cursor identity across calls) exposes it immediately. Decoys: the `islice` sample in `validation.py` and the `retry_call` factory re-invocation in `utils/retry.py`/`pipeline.py` (both correct given fresh iterators); `peek` in `csv_source.py` (correctly chains the first row back); `tee` used for two passes in `report.py`; `chunked`/`islice` in `storage.py`; module-level caches `_TABLES` in `fx.py` and `lru_cache` `_compile` in `categorize.py`; the `retrying` decorator in `app.list_feeds`; and CSV/JSONL sources that reopen their files per `records()` call. The small `nova` feed also works under validation because its whole feed fits in the preflight sample, so the cursor does get closed.
